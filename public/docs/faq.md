# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## Can other players see my custom skin/cape?
It depends on what you are using:
- **Normal 64x64 Skins (Uploaded to Mojang via "Upload Java"):** Visible to **EVERYONE**, even players without the mod! You may need to Disconnect and Reconnect to the server for vanilla players to see the update.
- **HD Skins (128x128+) & Custom 3D Geometry:** Only visible to players who **also have the Better Skin mod installed**.

## Can I use `.mcpack` files directly from Bedrock Edition?
Better Skin can read `.pck` files directly if you place them in the `skin_packs/` folder. For `.mcpack` files, rename the file to `.zip`, extract the folder inside, and place that folder into `skin_packs/`.

## Why is my skin/cape not updating for my friends?
Minecraft caches player profiles. If you change your skin while already in a server, your friends will still see your old skin. To fix this, simply **Disconnect and Reconnect** to the server. You do NOT need to close the entire game.

## Do I need Fabric API or any other mod?
- **Fabric:** Yes, you need **Fabric API** installed alongside Better Skin.
- **NeoForge:** No extra dependencies needed! Just drop the Better Skin `.jar` into your `mods/` folder and you're good to go.

## My skin list is empty! Is the mod broken?
No! Your skin list is empty because you haven't added any Skin Packs yet. See [Download & Install Skin Packs](download-and-install-skin-packs.md) for how to download ready-made packs, or [Create Your Own Skin Packs](create-your-own-skin-packs.md) for how to make your own.

## Are transparent (invisible) skins allowed?
The mod supports transparency in textures, but server admins may consider fully invisible skins as cheating. Please respect server rules.
